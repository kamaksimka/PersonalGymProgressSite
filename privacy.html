Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 26, 2026 · Application: Personal Gym Progress

Personal Gym Progress is an offline-first workout tracking application. Your privacy is central to how the app is built: we do not operate backend servers, we do not run third-party advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell or share your personal data.

1. Data Stored on Your Device

All training data you enter in Personal Gym Progress is stored locally on your device, including:

  • Workout logs, dates, names, and exercise entries;
  • Set metrics (weight, repetitions, duration, distance, incline, speed, RPE) and notes;
  • Photos and videos you choose to attach to sets;
  • App preferences (theme, language, weight unit).

2. Google User Data & Google Drive Synchronization

Cloud backup is completely optional. If you choose to connect a Google account in Settings → Synchronization, the app uses Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API with the following scope:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata — grants access strictly to a hidden, application-specific folder (appDataFolder) in your Google Drive.

When Google Drive Synchronization is enabled, the app accesses and uses:

  • Google Account Email & Account ID: used solely to display the currently connected account in the app's synchronization settings.
  • Google Drive App Data Folder: used solely to upload, update, list, and download your Personal Gym Progress backup files (your workout database and attached media) between your device and your own Google Drive account.

Personal Gym Progress cannot access, read, modify, or delete any other files or folders in your Google Drive.

3. Data Sharing, Storage & Limited Use

  • Your workout data and backups remain on your device and inside your own Google Drive appDataFolder.
  • We do not have access to your Google Drive files, and we do not transmit your Google user data to any external developer servers.
  • We do not share, sell, rent, or transfer Google user data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising, profiling, or AI/ML model training.
  • Personal Gym Progress's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Retention & Revoking Access

  • You can sign out and disconnect your Google account at any time inside the app (Settings → Synchronization).
  • You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account Permissions.
  • You can delete your app backup data from Google Drive via Google Drive settings (Manage Apps → Personal Gym Progress → Delete hidden app data) or uninstall the app to remove local data from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Personal Gym Progress is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
