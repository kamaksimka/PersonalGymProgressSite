Optionally connect your Google account to back up and restore your workout database and attachments inside your private Google Drive App Data folder (drive.appdata).

How Google Drive Backup Works

Personal Gym Progress works 100% offline by default. All workouts, exercises, and media stay on your device unless you choose to enable Google Drive Synchronization in Settings → Synchronization.

When enabled, the app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope. This restricts access strictly to a hidden, app-specific folder in your Google Drive — the app cannot see, read, or modify any other files in your Drive.
